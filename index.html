About This Dish

Sate Ayam Gunungkidul is a beloved Indonesian dish that originates from the Gunungkidul regency in Yogyakarta. This traditional chicken satay is distinguished by its unique sweet and savory marinade, which combines aromatic spices with the richness of sweet soy sauce.

The chicken is carefully marinated, then grilled over charcoal to achieve that perfect smoky flavor and caramelized exterior. Served with a generous helping of thick peanut sauce, this dish represents the heart of Javanese culinary tradition.

How to Prepare

Cut chicken breast into small cubes (approximately 2cm each)

Grind shallots, garlic, coriander seeds, and turmeric into a smooth paste

Mix the paste with sweet soy sauce, palm sugar, and lime juice

Marinate chicken cubes in the mixture for at least 2 hours (preferably overnight)

Thread marinated chicken onto bamboo skewers (5-6 pieces per skewer)

Grill over charcoal or in a grill pan, turning frequently and basting with remaining marinade

Serve hot with peanut sauce, sliced cucumber, tomato, and rice cakes (lontong)

Chef's Tips

Perfect Marinade

For best results, marinate the chicken overnight. This allows the spices to fully penetrate the meat, resulting in more flavorful satay.

Grilling Technique

Use charcoal for authentic smoky flavor. Keep the heat medium-high and turn frequently to prevent burning while ensuring even cooking.

Peanut Sauce

Make your peanut sauce from scratch using roasted peanuts, garlic, chili, and sweet soy sauce for the most authentic taste.

Serving Suggestion

Traditionally served with lontong (rice cakes), sliced cucumber, tomatoes, and fried shallots for a complete meal experience.
